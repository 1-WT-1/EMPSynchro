[center][b]EMP Synchrotrons[/b][/center]

Equipment mod for ΔV: Rings of Saturn.

[b]What if your mining weapon fired lightning storms?[/b]

This mod introduces the experimental weapon "Bremsstrahlung" - that infuses gigawatts of EMP potential directly into ringoids to gently separate ores from clusters. Dealing massive electromagnetic damage upon impact, it leaves a highly dangerous residual charge in the immediate area.
